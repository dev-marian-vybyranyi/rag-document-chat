Engineering Manager
Company: Globex

About the role
Globex builds logistics software. The engineering manager leads one team of eight engineers and reports to the head of engineering.

Requirements
- Technical background in backend development
- Experience leading a team of engineers
- Hiring and interviewing engineers
- Roadmap planning
- 7+ years of professional experience

Responsibilities
Lead the team day to day and plan its roadmap with product. Hire new engineers and help the current ones grow. Remove what blocks the team.
